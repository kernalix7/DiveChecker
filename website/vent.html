PROBLEM

Trying Mouthfill for the first time
right before your deep dive?

01

Nowhere to practice it

Mouthfill only matters past ~25-30m, where Frenzel from the lungs stops working. Pools are too shallow; every ocean attempt costs a full dive. So most divers end up doing it for real before they've drilled it.

02

No way to know if it worked

Is your T-block sealed? Is the tongue piston actually generating pressure? No objective way to check until depth — and by then you either get the dive, or abort it.

03

A failed dive is dangerous

Mouthfill failure at depth means an immediate ascent. Skipped practice puts the entire dive at risk.

HOW IT WORKS

How the balloon validates Mouthfill

The balloon only inflates past a threshold. Visual confirmation that your Mouthfill compression generated enough pressure

1

Seal the nose piece on one nostril

Pinch the opposite nostril shut. Make sure the balloon is firmly seated on the nose piece end of the cap.

2

Charge → T-block → tongue piston

Take air into your mouth (charge), close off the throat to isolate from the lungs (T-block), then push that air into the nasal passage with your tongue and soft palate (tongue piston). Valsalva won't inflate the balloon.

3

Grapefruit size = success

Hold each inflation for 2~3 seconds, alternate nostrils, 5 sets. Daily repetition builds the tongue-piston muscle pattern.
