General

DiveChecker is a real-time pressure monitoring device for freediving equalization training. It measures subtle pressure changes from your nose/mouth at 100Hz using a high-precision pressure sensor (BMP280) and visualizes them as real-time graphs through a dedicated app.

DiveChecker Vent

Vent is a dry-land Mouthfill trainer with a precisely calibrated balloon. Valsalva or simple lung exhalation won't inflate it — only the charge → T-block → tongue piston motion will. While the DiveChecker sensor measures and visualizes your pressure curves, Vent forces the correct motion in the first place. Used together, they close the learning loop.

Vent is for freedivers preparing for deep dives (typically 25-30m+). Past that depth, your lungs are compressed enough that Frenzel-from-the-lungs stops working. If your dives are shallower than that, the DiveChecker sensor is enough for Frenzel/Valsalva training. Pick up Vent when your next target goes past 30m.

Yes, Vent works standalone — the inflated balloon is your visual feedback. Pairing it with the DiveChecker sensor adds quantitative pressure-curve analysis for finer technique correction, which is why we recommend the kit.

Otovent is a medical autoinflation device with a relatively low threshold (just enough to pop your ears). DiveChecker Vent is recalibrated to Mouthfill compression pressure levels, with an ergonomic nose piece — built specifically for freediving. With Otovent the balloon inflates with Valsalva too, which can reinforce bad habits; with Vent it won't.
